# Investment Readiness Criteria Index

**Status:** Draft  
**Version:** 0.1.0-draft

Terms and acronyms are defined in the [glossary](03-glossary.md).

This document is the normative public index of the **25 criteria** across five dimensions. Each criterion is a diligence domain commonly reviewed by institutional investors.

For score architecture and bands, see [01-methodology.md](01-methodology.md).

---

## Why this index is public

Open standards such as Technology Readiness Levels (TRL), a public vocabulary that originated at NASA, became useful because a shared vocabulary was published and adopted across organizations. This proposal invites review of a shared public index for investment readiness and diligence posture.

This criteria index is introduced as an **industry vocabulary in formation**. Practitioners are invited to critique names, scope, and gaps through Issues and requests for comments (RFCs).

## 3. Evidence categories (informative)

1. Structured self-reported inputs  
2. Documentary artifacts (statements, agreements, certificates, contracts)  
3. System records from operational tools (banking, accounting, billing, customer relationship management (CRM), payroll, cap table)  
4. Cross-checks between claims and records  

Vendor product names are not normative. Any system that produces auditable records may serve as evidence.

---

## 4. Financial Health (FH)

### FH-01 · Financial Record-Keeping & Reporting
- **Measurement scope:** Accrual accounting practice, monthly close cadence, and statement preparation (profit and loss (P&L), balance sheet, cash flow).
- **Typical evidence:** Accounting system records or statements reviewed by a certified public accountant (CPA).

### FH-02 · Revenue Trend & Recognition
- **Measurement scope:** Consistency of recurring revenue (monthly recurring revenue (MRR) or annual recurring revenue (ARR) where applicable), revenue recognition discipline, separation of one-time vs recurring revenue.
- **Typical evidence:** Billing/subscription ledgers or audited revenue schedules.

### FH-03 · Cash Flow & Runway Management
- **Measurement scope:** Burn rates, zero-cash-date accuracy, and working-capital discipline.
- **Typical evidence:** Banking records and short-horizon cash forecasts.

### FH-04 · Unit Economics & Key Metrics
- **Measurement scope:** customer acquisition cost (CAC), customer lifetime value (LTV), gross margin, and payback period rigor where applicable to the model.
- **Typical evidence:** Cost and revenue records that support unit-economic calculations.

### FH-05 · Financial Projections & Modeling
- **Measurement scope:** Bottom-up driver modeling, scenario analysis, and actuals-to-budget variance tracking.
- **Typical evidence:** Documented models and historical variance analysis.

## 5. Operational Maturity (OM)

### OM-01 · Leadership Team & Key-Person Risk
- **Measurement scope:** Role completeness, key-person dependency, and continuity if a founder is unavailable.
- **Typical evidence:** Org records, employment/equity documentation.

### OM-02 · Operational Process & Cadence
- **Measurement scope:** Operating procedures, meeting discipline, and work-management rigor.
- **Typical evidence:** Written procedures and active operating systems.

### OM-03 · Product Infrastructure & Scalability
- **Measurement scope:** Infrastructure resilience, recovery planning, and security posture appropriate to the product.
- **Typical evidence:** Architecture notes, uptime/status history, security documentation.

### OM-04 · Quality & Customer Support Operations
- **Measurement scope:** Support service-level agreements (SLAs), resolution discipline, quality assurance (QA) practices, escalation paths.
- **Typical evidence:** Support metrics and release/QA records.

### OM-05 · KPI Cadence & Performance Management
- **Measurement scope:** Centralized KPI tracking and regular performance review discipline.
- **Typical evidence:** Dashboard exports and review archives.

---

## 6. Market Position (MP)

### MP-01 · Target Customer Definition (ICP)
- **Measurement scope:** ICP precision, economic buyer clarity, and quantified pain.
- **Typical evidence:** ICP documentation and segmented customer records.

### MP-02 · Product-Market Fit Validation
- **Measurement scope:** Retention conviction, usage, willingness-to-pay, and customer signal quality.
- **Typical evidence:** Cohort data, product analytics, customer research.

### MP-03 · Competitive Advantage & Moats
- **Measurement scope:** Defensibility, switching costs, proprietary assets, positioning vs alternatives.
- **Typical evidence:** Win/loss records, IP filings, comparative analyses.

### MP-04 · Go-to-Market Execution
- **Measurement scope:** Repeatable acquisition channels, pipeline conversion, sales process standardization.
- **Typical evidence:** CRM pipeline analytics and channel attribution.

### MP-05 · Addressable Headroom & TAM Reality
- **Measurement scope:** Bottom-up market sizing grounded in buyer counts and realistic contract value.
- **Typical evidence:** Auditable sizing models tied to pricing and account data.

---

## 7. Governance & Compliance (GC)

### GC-01 · Entity Structure & Governance
- **Measurement scope:** Clean formation, good standing, and basic board/governance hygiene.
- **Typical evidence:** Formation documents, organizational consents, filings.

### GC-02 · IP Assignment & Ownership Integrity
- **Measurement scope:** Contributor IP assignment completeness and ownership clarity.
- **Typical evidence:** Executed assignment agreements and related IP records.

### GC-03 · Cap Table Structure & Equity Controls
- **Measurement scope:** Formal capitalization records, convertible instruments, vesting, option plan hygiene.
- **Typical evidence:** Cap-table system records and equity plan documents.

### GC-04 · Regulatory & Legal Compliance
- **Measurement scope:** Tax, worker classification, privacy, and industry-specific obligations as applicable.
- **Typical evidence:** Filings, policies, and compliance records.

### GC-05 · Corporate Insurance & Risk Mitigation
- **Measurement scope:** Stage-appropriate insurance and risk controls.
- **Typical evidence:** Certificates of insurance and risk registers.

---

## 8. Traction (TR)

### TR-01 · Customer Growth & Revenue Velocity
- **Measurement scope:** Growth in revenue and/or customers and pipeline conversion velocity.
- **Typical evidence:** Billing growth trends and validated revenue ledgers.

### TR-02 · Customer Retention & Cohort Health
- **Measurement scope:** net revenue retention (NRR) and gross revenue retention (GRR), logo retention, cohort churn dynamics where applicable.
- **Typical evidence:** Cohort retention analytics and renewal records.

### TR-03 · Commercial Pilot & Deployment Validation
- **Measurement scope:** Pilot success criteria, pilot-to-paid conversion, time-to-value.
- **Typical evidence:** Pilot agreements and conversion records.

### TR-04 · Strategic Partnerships & Channel Distribution
- **Measurement scope:** Partner agreements, distribution commitments, partner-attributed revenue.
- **Typical evidence:** Signed agreements and attribution records.

### TR-05 · Market Velocity & Ecosystem Recognition
- **Measurement scope:** Non-dilutive awards, industry recognition, and credible external momentum signals.
- **Typical evidence:** Award documentation and verified third-party recognition.
