# Public assessment review workflow

Terms and acronyms are defined in the [glossary](03-glossary.md).

This workflow tests the specification's clarity, applicability and evidence coverage. It does not calculate an IRS or disclose implementation rules.

| Phase | Inputs | Activities | Deliverables |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Scope | Specification version; company context; reviewer role; authorization | Identify applicable criteria and review purpose | Completed context section and applicability notes |
| 2. Describe | Public criteria and permitted claim descriptions | Record what each relevant claim means; flag ambiguity | Criterion worksheet |
| 3. Map evidence | Evidence categories and nonconfidential descriptions | Identify available, missing and contradictory material | Evidence availability and limitation notes |
| 4. Review findings | Worksheet and supporting references | Identify gaps, competing interpretations and practical consequences | Findings with criterion identifiers |
| 5. Submit feedback | Findings; proposed wording if available | Remove confidential details; choose issue or RFC route | Review submission linked to the evaluated version |
| 6. Disposition | Submission and maintainer review | Accept, defer, reject or request clarification, with reasons | Comment disposition and any linked change proposal |

Reviewers MUST identify the specification version. They MUST NOT upload confidential company evidence or invent a score. Proposed replacement wording is helpful but is not a condition for raising a valid concern.

If evidence cannot be shared, describe its category and the problem without exposing its contents. If applicability is uncertain, record the uncertainty. Do not silently omit it or assign zero points.

Use [the worksheet](../templates/field-review-worksheet.md) and [case record](../templates/case-study-record.md).
