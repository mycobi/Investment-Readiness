# Feedback routing and acknowledgment

Terms and acronyms are defined in the [glossary](03-glossary.md).

Specific defects, clarification requests and field observations use structured Issues. Broad conceptual questions use [Discussions](https://github.com/mycobi/Investment-Readiness/discussions), which is enabled. People outside MYCOBI propose edits from a fork. Only MYCOBI maintainers merge. Proposed edits use pull requests.

Vulnerabilities and other confidential concerns go to support@mycobi.com. See [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md). Do not post sensitive information in public issues, pull requests, or discussions.

Every Issue form requires the specification version and an affected file, section or criterion. A new-domain proposal may identify the nearest related criterion and explain that it proposes new scope.

Replacement wording is optional. A reviewer may identify ambiguity, inconsistent claims or missing evidence without proving operational harm. Explain the concern sufficiently for another person to locate and assess it.

## From submission to decision

1. Submit through the appropriate form.
2. A maintainer acknowledges a substantive submission within five business days during the review window, following GOVERNANCE.md.
3. The maintainer identifies the review route and requests clarification if needed.
4. A proposed material change uses an RFC; an editorial change may proceed directly to a PR.
5. Original material offered for incorporation follows the CLA process. Criticism alone needs no agreement.
6. An independent authorized reviewer examines proposed changes.
7. Record the disposition and rationale in DISPOSITION-OF-COMMENTS.md and link the resulting change, if any.

Discussions and Issues are enabled. These documents do not record a completed public test of the issue-to-pull-request route. No test submission or simulated disposition is represented as community feedback. Original material offered for incorporation still requires the CLA acceptance in [CONTRIBUTOR-ACCEPTANCE.md](../CONTRIBUTOR-ACCEPTANCE.md). No CLA bot is live.
