# Change log

## 0.1.0-draft (published 28 October 2026)

First public draft, opening the six-month review from 28 October 2026 to 28 April 2027.

- Five dimensions and 25 public criteria, with 200 points per dimension and 1,000 overall. Five score ranges are set; their labels are open for comment.
- Methodology with related work, the evidence-provenance rationale, limitations and a competing-interests statement.
- Glossary, public scope and evidence boundary, assessment review workflow and feedback process.
- Review roadmap: draft freeze on 28 February 2027, monthly digests on the 28th from 28 November 2026 through 28 April 2027, and comments closing at 11:59 p.m. America/New_York on 28 April 2027.
- Issue forms, RFC template, comment disposition register and field-testing templates, including a clearly hypothetical walkthrough.
- Governance with designated reviewers, contributing guide, CLA version 1.2 and a private contact route at support@mycobi.com.
