# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Investment Readiness standard a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the standard and its adopters

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- Publishing others’ private information without permission
- Sharing confidential company diligence data in Issues or PRs
- Conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately by email to support@mycobi.com. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for vulnerabilities and other confidential concerns. Do not post sensitive details in public issues, pull requests, or discussions. Maintainers will review and respond as appropriate, including warnings or removal from participation.
