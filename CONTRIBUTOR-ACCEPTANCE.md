# Contributor acceptance

Acceptances are recorded through hosted CLA Assistant. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for its current status and the interim process.

## Ordinary individual contributions

1. Open a pull request and follow the CLA Assistant link when the service is configured.
2. Read the full agreement presented by the service and accept from your own account.
3. Acceptance of version 1.2 dated 2026-09-09 covers that submission and later Contributions intentionally offered under that version. You need not post a new acceptance comment on every PR.
4. Accept again when the agreement changes. Existing agreements are not silently amended.

Questions and criticism require no signature. Original material proposed for incorporation does. For material first offered in an issue or RFC, maintainers must include its author in the acceptance review when that material is incorporated. A passing CLA check alone may not identify authors whose wording was copied into a commit by someone else.

## Exceptions

Entity contributions and employer-owned work require authority to grant the rights. Contact an authorized maintainer at support@mycobi.com before incorporation if separate paperwork is needed. Private identity details and signed documents must not be posted publicly.

Until the service is active, incorporation requires a separately verified explicit acceptance identifying the exact agreement version and contribution scope. Do not treat an unchecked or prefilled box, a commit sign-off, or another person's statement as acceptance.

Maintainers retain the account, accepted agreement version, date and service record, or a private signed-agreement reference. Earlier version 1.1 records keep their original scope. See [CLA](CLA.md).
