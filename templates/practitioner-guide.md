# Practitioner review guide

This kit tests the public IRD and IRS specification's clarity, coverage and usefulness. It does not calculate an IRS or test private scoring logic.

## Prepare

Record the specification version and review purpose. Use a company you are authorized to review, or the explicitly hypothetical walkthrough supplied here. Keep source records and identifying information outside public feedback.

Read the [criteria index](../docs/02-criteria-index.md) and [evidence categories](../docs/04-public-scope-and-evidence.md). Do not assume every criterion applies equally to every company.

## Complete the review

1. Copy the [worksheet](field-review-worksheet.md).
2. For each selected criterion, describe the claim, applicability and evidence types available. State which criteria were not reviewed.
3. Separate missing information, contradictory information and an observed weakness. Do not convert these findings into points.
4. Record what the public wording helped you assess and what required interpretation.
5. Complete the [findings report](findings-report.md). Recommend an information request or process improvement without implying funding eligibility.
6. Submit a sanitized observation through the field-review Issue form. Cite the version and criterion. Proposed replacement wording is optional.
7. Use [Discussions](https://github.com/mycobi/Investment-Readiness/discussions), which is enabled, for a broader conceptual question. A material specification change proceeds through an RFC and maintainer decision. Report vulnerabilities or confidential concerns to support@mycobi.com. See [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md). Do not put them in a public issue.

See the [completed illustrative walkthrough](illustrative-walkthrough.md). Its facts and outcomes are constructed solely to demonstrate the process. They are not validation findings.

## Completion criteria

A usable review identifies its scope, evidence types, limitations, findings and next actions. Another reviewer should understand the reasoning without access to proprietary score calculations. No score, band or financing recommendation is needed.

Questions and criticism require no CLA. Original text offered for incorporation follows [contributor acceptance](../CONTRIBUTOR-ACCEPTANCE.md).
