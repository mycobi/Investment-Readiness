# Completed illustrative practitioner walkthrough

**Hypothetical training example.** Every company detail, record description and finding below is constructed to demonstrate use of the public kit. No company was evaluated, no records were inspected, and no empirical validation is claimed.

## 1. Context and scope

Specification: 0.1.0-draft. Example company: an unnamed subscription software business with paying customers and a founder-led operating team. Purpose: test whether reviewers can describe diligence gaps using public criteria and evidence categories.

The exercise selects FH-01, OM-01, MP-01, GC-02 and TR-02, one criterion per dimension. The other 20 criteria are not reviewed. This is a completed scoped review, not a complete diagnostic.

## 2. Assumed inputs and completed worksheet

| Criterion | Assumed claim | Evidence types described in this scenario | Finding | Next action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FH-01 | Financial statements are prepared monthly. | Documentary statements and accounting system records are described, but no record of review or reconciliation is supplied. | Missing evidence of the stated review process. This does not establish inaccurate accounts. | Ask for a description and supporting record of the monthly review process. |
| OM-01 | The business can operate during the founder's absence. | Structured self-report and a role chart identify the founder as the only person responsible for customer escalation. | The scenario contains an apparent dependency requiring clarification. Other continuity arrangements are unknown. | Ask who can assume the responsibility and what continuity arrangements exist. |

Market demand and commercial success are not established. | Record the limited finding and identify any unreviewed customer segments. |
| GC-02 | All contributor IP assignments are documented. | The self-report says all are documented; the described document inventory leaves one contributor's agreement status unresolved. | An unresolved discrepancy between the claim and inventory. Missing documentation does not prove missing legal ownership. | Ask for clarification of the contributor's agreement status through an appropriate reviewer. |
| TR-02 | Customer retention is tracked consistently. | Two documentary summaries use different time periods without stating those periods prominently. | The summaries cannot be compared as presented. No conclusion about actual retention quality follows. | Request consistent period descriptions before interpreting the trend. |

No automatic threshold, proof recipe, score adjustment or funding band follows from these observations.

## 3. Completed findings report

The selected criteria provide a place to record financial reporting, continuity, customer definition, ownership documentation and retention questions. The useful output is a set of bounded findings rather than a score.

In this constructed scenario, the principal information requests concern financial review documentation, continuity responsibilities, contributor agreement status and retention reporting periods. The target-customer descriptions are internally consistent as described, but no independent verification occurred.

The exercise leaves the rest of the diagnostic unreviewed. It does not establish the company's overall readiness or the framework's reliability.

## 4. Example feedback submission

**Type:** Clarification.

**Location:** Version 0.1.0-draft, docs/02-criteria-index.md, TR-02.

**Concern:** The criterion lists cohort retention analytics but does not explain how a reviewer should communicate that two reports cover different periods. The public review guide could help reviewers preserve that limitation.

**Suggested wording, for discussion only:** Describe the reporting period and any limitation that prevents comparison.

**Requested outcome:** Maintainer clarification about whether the review guide already addresses this concern. No change to the private scoring method is requested.
