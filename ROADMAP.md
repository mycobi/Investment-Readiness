# Six-month RFC roadmap

**Confirmed start:** 28 October 2026.
**Six-month close:** 28 April 2027.
**Phase boundaries below:** The six-month shape was confirmed by the steward on 10 September 2026. It moved with the start on 25 September 2026 and again to this 28 October 2026 start. No phase is tied to an external event in these documents.
**Draft freeze:** 28 February 2027.

| Phase | Dates | Focus | Output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery | 28 October to 27 December 2026 | Terminology, scope, criteria, public/private boundary, band interpretation | Classified comments and prioritized proposals |
| Field Testing | 28 December 2026 to 27 February 2027 | Practitioner use of public worksheets; ambiguity and evidence availability | Documented field observations and candidate revisions |
| Draft Freeze | 28 February to 27 March 2027 | Resolve accepted changes; check consistency and migration implications | Frozen candidate with outstanding objections listed |
| Ratification review | 28 March to 28 April 2027 | Review dispositions and unresolved blockers | Steward decision to release, extend review, or defer |

Ratification here means a MYCOBI steward decision on this draft. It does not mean approval by an external standards organization.

## Review milestones

Monthly review digests: 28 November and 28 December 2026; 28 January, 28 February, 28 March and 28 April 2027. The first digest is 28 November 2026, one month after the review opens, and later digests stay on the 28th through the close. Each digest should list received comments, dispositions, changes and unresolved decisions. No digest is claimed as published yet.

The submission cutoff is 11:59 p.m. America/New_York on 28 April 2027. This administrative cutoff uses the project working timezone. Later comments remain welcome for the next revision; serious defects may reopen the current review.

## Freeze policy

Major structural rewrites SHOULD NOT be introduced during the final two months (28 February to 28 April 2027). A serious defect may justify reopening the affected scope or extending review. The decision and reason MUST be recorded; the calendar must not force adoption of a known defect.

Community triage-call dates and reviewer assignments are not set. See OPEN-DECISIONS.md. This roadmap does not itself make the repository public or activate contributor services.
